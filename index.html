What to know about the work, the timeline, and your investment.

What are SEO, GEO, and AEO?+

SEO helps your business be discovered in search engines and Maps. GEO means generative engine optimization, focused on visibility in AI-generated answers. AEO means answer engine optimization, focused on answering questions clearly. They are connected approaches, with SEO providing the foundation.

Why should my business invest in search visibility?+

People research salons, treatments, dentists, home repairs, and remodeling before they book. Maintaining a useful website, accurate local listings, and clear service information belongs in every service business's marketing plan. The right level of investment depends on your goals and competition.

How much time should I commit?+

Our core SEO packages require a six-month minimum. Plan for 6-12 months of consistent work to give your SEO and GEO presence time to develop. This is an investment horizon, not a promise of a particular ranking or number of leads.

How long will it take to rank for my keywords?+

There is no fixed deadline. Your site's history, technical condition, local competition, proximity, and the specificity of your keywords all affect progress. Some changes may show earlier movement; competitive phrases may take longer than 12 months. We review progress and adjust priorities rather than guarantee positions.

Do specific keywords rank faster than broad ones?+

A phrase such as 'balayage specialist in [neighborhood]' or 'heat pump repair in [city]' may face less competition than 'hair salon' or 'HVAC.' Specific searches can also indicate stronger intent. We assess the actual market before choosing targets; specificity alone does not guarantee a faster ranking.

Can paid ads bring leads sooner?+

Google Ads, Meta campaigns, and lead ads can create visibility sooner once approved and launched. They require budget, relevant offers, good follow-up, and optimization. Leads are not guaranteed, and advertising does not buy higher organic rankings. Management and ad spend are separate from SEO pricing.

Which industries do you work with?+

Hair salons and specialists, aesthetics and medspas, dental practices, HVAC companies, and contracting or remodeling businesses. Service pages, questions, keywords, and conversion paths are tailored to each business. Medical and dental content needs review by the appropriate qualified professional.

Is the local-ranking map a real result?+

The map on this page is an illustrative example. A real scan would need your business listing, keyword, service area, and a connected ranking-data provider. No example ranking is presented as a client result.

What does the free audit commit me to?+

Nothing paid starts from an audit request. Scope, fees, and commitment are agreed before paid work begins. Submit your website and service area through the form to request a review.
